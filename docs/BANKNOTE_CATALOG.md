# Banknote catalog

Checked on 8 October 2026 against the primary sources below. The catalog covers 32 currencies and contains documented denominations, not a promise to cover every older or commemorative series. GBP covers Bank of England notes; CNY covers fifth-series yuan notes; IDR covers the standard 2022 series; SGD covers the Portrait Series including the still-valid high denominations.

Unverified currencies use a clearly labelled indicative table. Generated or legacy example amounts cannot be added as banknotes. Previous counts remain visible and removable, including denominations no longer listed.
