Moneta is a currency converter for travellers, with offline rates, a banknote counter and no ads.

<b>Works offline, including the first launch</b>
Reference rates ship inside the app. Your cached rates, cash table and banknote counts stay available without a connection. The date of the rate remains visible.

<b>Shows the real age of each conversion</b>
The rate date is separate from the time the provider was checked. A cross-rate uses the oldest quotation it needs. Dates older than seven days are shown in red; unknown dates are stated explicitly.

<b>Multiple sources and controlled updates</b>
Moneta tries other sources when a provider fails or lacks your currencies. Each conversion uses a complete snapshot from one source. Choose automatic intervals, manual-only, Wi-Fi-only or offline mode. Wi-Fi-only also applies to manual requests. A compatible self-hosted Frankfurter v2 endpoint can be saved and verified separately.

<b>Made for travel</b>
• Compact numeric keypad; expand the calculator for expressions
• Cash exchange fees reduce what you receive; card fees increase the cost
• Copy and paste amounts in your app language
• Saved amounts, expressions and active field
• Favourite currencies in your own order, with dragging and accessible move controls
• Independent Home/Local cash pair, real banknote denominations and banknote counting
• Counts saved per local currency; totals always available in both currencies
• Historical charts and local-currency suggestions without location permission

<b>Decimal arithmetic</b>
Amounts and rates use BigDecimal, with 16 significant digits and HALF_UP in intermediate operations, plus currency-aware display rounding. Repeating decimal results have finite precision. These are reference rates, not a promise of the amount a bank or bureau will charge.

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, trackers or analytics. No Google Play Services. Internet and network-state access are used for rates and your network preferences; no location permission is needed.
