Honesty fixes, all found by using the app.

• Refreshing with no network no longer claims success. The cache was answering from disk without reaching the source, so old rates got a fresh timestamp — the one thing this app must never get wrong.
• The last row of the keypad can no longer be pushed off screen by the card fee line.
• "Max" really shows twenty years now, instead of whatever shorter range had been viewed before.
• The chart says which source the numbers come from, and the period actually covered.
• The currencies board gets its screen back: a compact numeric keypad that can also be collapsed.
• New app icon, the denarius of Juno Moneta.
